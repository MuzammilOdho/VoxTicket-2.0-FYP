Tracking: once an order ships, a shipment record with a carrier and tracking number becomes available.
Delivery attempts: if a delivery attempt fails, the carrier generally retries or leaves pickup instructions.
Lost shipments: a shipment marked LOST should be escalated as a claim rather than treated as a normal delay.
Delivered but not received: if a shipment shows DELIVERED but the customer says they did not receive it, this requires a delivery investigation and must not be automatically refunded.
Multiple shipments: a single order can have more than one shipment.
